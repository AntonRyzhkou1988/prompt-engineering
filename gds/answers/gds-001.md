The space missions dataset contains the following columns:

* **Company:** The company responsible for the space mission.
* **Location:** The launch location.
* **Date:** The launch date (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time:** The launch time (UTC).
* **Rocket:** The name of the rocket used.
* **Mission:** The name of the mission.
* **RocketStatus:** The status of the rocket (e.g., "Retired" or "Active").
* **Price:** The cost of the rocket in millions of US dollars.
* **MissionStatus:** The outcome of the mission (e.g., "Success," "Failure").
