## Section A: Summary of Findings

### Fatalities
- **No fatalities** were recorded in the provided data. All incidents were non-fatal.  
  Confidence: High

### Activities
- **Surfing** was the most common activity associated with shark encounters, followed by **spearfishing** and **body boarding**.  
  Confidence: High

### Injury Types
- Injuries ranged from **minor lacerations** and **bruises** to **severe bites** and **amputations**. The most severe injury recorded was a **severed right forearm**.  
  Confidence: High

### Shark Species
- The species involved in the encounters included **White shark**, **7 gill shark**, **Tiger shark**, and **smooth hound**. Some incidents did not specify the shark species.  
  Confidence: Medium

## Section B: Patterns and Trends

### Geographic Distribution
- Shark encounters were reported in **Australia**, **USA**, **Bahamas**, **United Kingdom**, and **United Arab Emirates**.  
  Confidence: High

### Time of Day
- Encounters occurred at various times of the day, with some incidents specifying **morning** or **specific hours** like **08h30** and **15h45**.  
  Confidence: Medium

### Provoked vs. Unprovoked
- Most encounters were **unprovoked**. Only one incident was explicitly marked as **provoked** (touching a shark).  
  Confidence: High

## Section C: Data Quality and Ambiguities

### Missing Values
- Several records had missing values for **Age**, **Time**, and **Species**.  
  Confidence: High

### Ambiguities
- The term **"board bitten"** and **"knocked off board"** in injury descriptions could benefit from more precise definitions to understand the severity of the encounters.  
  Confidence: Medium

## Section D: Recommendations for Future Data Collection

### Standardization
- Implement standardized categories for **Injury** descriptions to ensure clarity and consistency in severity assessment.

### Completeness
- Ensure all fields, especially **Age**, **Time**, and **Species**, are filled out to provide a comprehensive understanding of each encounter.

### Detailed Reporting
- Encourage detailed reporting on the circumstances of each encounter, including the behavior of the shark and the actions of the individuals involved.

### Geographic Precision
- Provide more precise geographic locations within the **Area** field to better understand regional patterns and risks.

By addressing these recommendations, future data collection can improve the accuracy and utility of shark encounter records.
