# Shark Encounter Analysis

## Section A: Incident Summary
The dataset includes 10 records of shark encounters from the year 2017 across various countries. The incidents are categorized by type, activity, injury, and other relevant details.

## Section B: Key Findings

### 1. **Fatalities**
- **Finding:** There are no fatalities reported in the provided records.
  - **Confidence:** High

### 2. **Types of Encounters**
- **Finding:** The majority of encounters are unprovoked (8 out of 10).
  - **Confidence:** High
  - **Details:** 
    - Unprovoked incidents include activities such as body boarding, surfing, spearfishing, snorkeling, and feeding sharks.
    - Provoked incidents include touching a shark and fishing.

### 3. **Activities During Encounters**
- **Finding:** Surfing and spearfishing are common activities during shark encounters.
  - **Confidence:** High
  - **Details:** 
    - Surfing: 3 incidents (Australia, USA, United Kingdom)
    - Spearfishing: 2 incidents (USA, United Arab Emirates)

### 4. **Injuries**
- **Finding:** Injuries vary from minor abrasions to severe bites and severed limbs.
  - **Confidence:** High
  - **Details:** 
    - Minor injuries: No injury, board bitten; knocked off board; bruise to leg, cuts to hand; abrasion to right forearm.
    - Severe injuries: Right forearm severed; right leg severely bitten.

### 5. **Species Involved**
- **Finding:** Various shark species are involved, with some records missing species identification.
  - **Confidence:** Medium
  - **Details:** 
    - Identified species: White shark, 7 gill shark, Tiger shark, Smooth hound.
    - Unidentified species: 3 records missing species information.

## Section C: Patterns and Trends

### 1. **Geographical Distribution**
- **Observation:** Encounters are reported from multiple countries, with the USA and Australia having the highest number of incidents.
  - **Confidence:** High
  - **Details:** 
    - USA: 4 incidents (Florida, South Carolina)
    - Australia: 3 incidents (Western Australia, Victoria, New South Wales)

### 2. **Time of Day**
- **Observation:** Encounters occur at various times of the day, with some records missing time information.
  - **Confidence:** Medium
  - **Details:** 
    - Morning: 2 incidents
    - Specific times: 08h30, 15h45, 10h00, shortly before 12h00
    - Missing time: 4 records

## Section D: Uncertainties and Limitations

### 1. **Missing Data**
- **Observation:** Several records have missing or incomplete data, particularly regarding age, time, and species.
  - **Confidence:** High
  - **Details:** 
    - Age: Missing in 3 records
    - Time: Missing in 4 records
    - Species: Missing in 3 records

### 2. **Ambiguity in Injury Descriptions**
- **Observation:** Injury descriptions vary in detail and clarity, which may affect the accuracy of severity assessments.
  - **Confidence:** Medium
  - **Details:** 
    - Example: "No injury, board bitten" vs. "Right forearm severed"

### 3. **Investigator Source Variability**
- **Observation:** The sources of investigation vary, which may affect the consistency and reliability of the data.
  - **Confidence:** Medium
  - **Details:** 
    - Sources include news outlets, GSAF, and individual investigators.
