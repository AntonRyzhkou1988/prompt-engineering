### Observations
- All incidents were non-fatal.
- Activities varied, with surfing and spearfishing being common.
- Injuries ranged from minor abrasions to severe bites and limb severance.
- Shark species identified included white shark, 7 gill shark, tiger shark, and smooth hound.
- The incidents occurred in diverse locations, including Australia, USA, Bahamas, United Kingdom, and United Arab Emirates.
