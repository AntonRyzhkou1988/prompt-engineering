## Shark Encounter Analysis for 2017

### Summary
In 2017, there were 10 recorded shark encounters across various countries. The majority were unprovoked incidents, with only one provoked encounter. None of the incidents were fatal.

### Incident Breakdown by Country
- **Australia**: 3 incidents
  - **Western Australia**: Body boarding, no injury (White shark, 4 m)
  - **Victoria**: Surfing, no injury (7 gill shark)
  - **New South Wales**: Fishing, abrasion from pectoral fin (Species unknown)
- **USA**: 4 incidents
  - **Florida**: Surfing, laceration to left foot (Species unknown)
  - **Florida**: Spearfishing, laceration to shin (8' shark)
  - **Florida**: Feeding sharks, lacerations to right arm (Tiger shark)
  - **South Carolina**: Touching a shark, right hand bitten (3' shark, provoked)
- **Bahamas**: 1 incident
  - **New Providence**: Snorkeling, right forearm severed (Tiger shark)
- **United Kingdom**: 1 incident
  - **South Devon**: Surfing, bruise to leg and cuts to hand (3m shark, probably a smooth hound)
- **United Arab Emirates**: 1 incident
  - **Sharjah**: Spearfishing, right leg severely bitten (Species unknown)
