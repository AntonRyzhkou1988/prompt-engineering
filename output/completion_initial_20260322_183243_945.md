## Section A: Shark Encounter Summary
In 2017, shark encounters were reported across various countries, including Australia, USA, United Kingdom, Bahamas, United Arab Emirates, Mexico, South Africa, Reunion, New Zealand, St Helena, and Indonesia. The activities during these encounters ranged from surfing, body boarding, snorkeling, spearfishing, fishing, and swimming. Injuries varied from minor lacerations to fatal incidents.

## Section B: Trends and Patterns
### Country and Area Analysis
- **Australia**: Multiple encounters were reported across Western Australia, Victoria, New South Wales, and Queensland. Activities included surfing, body boarding, snorkeling, and fishing.
- **USA**: Encounters were frequent in Florida and South Carolina, with activities such as surfing, swimming, and spearfishing.
- **South Africa**: Encounters in Western Cape Province and KwaZulu-Natal involved surfing and spearfishing.
- **Reunion**: Fatal encounters were reported during body boarding.
- **Bahamas**: Encounters during snorkeling and scuba diving were reported.

### Activity Analysis
- **Surfing**: Commonly reported in Australia, USA, and South Africa.
- **Body Boarding**: Reported in Australia, Reunion, and New Zealand.
- **Snorkeling**: Encounters in Bahamas and Australia.
- **Spearfishing**: Reported in USA, Australia, and South Africa.

### Injury Analysis
- **Fatal Encounters**: Reported in Reunion, Mexico, and Australia.
- **Non-Fatal Injuries**: Varied from minor lacerations to severe bites, commonly reported during surfing and spearfishing.

## Section C: Species Involved
- **White Shark**: Frequently involved in encounters in Australia and South Africa.
- **Tiger Shark**: Reported in Bahamas and Hawaii.
- **Bull Shark**: Involved in encounters in Australia and South Africa.
- **Other Species**: Includes Lemon Shark, Caribbean Reef Shark, and Seven-gill Shark.
